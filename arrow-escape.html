How to play

Tap an arrow to send it sliding off the board in the direction it points.

If the path to the edge is clear, it escapes. If another arrow is in the way, you lose a life. Three mistakes ends the run.

Clear every arrow to finish the level. Use hints to reveal a safe move, or undo your last successful move.
